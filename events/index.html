                                            Key dates from Elections BC

                                            • ◆ Thursday, October 1

                                              Register or update your voter information to make sure you receive a Where to Vote card.

                                              Source: Elections BC: key dates

                                            • ◆ Saturday, October 3

                                              Candidate nominations close at 1 p.m. (Pacific time).

                                              Source: Elections BC: key dates

                                            • ◆ Monday, October 12

                                              Thanksgiving Day: district electoral offices closed.

                                              Source: Elections BC: key dates · Elections BC: ways to vote

                                            • ◆ Friday, October 16 to Wednesday, October 21

                                              Advance voting, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. (local time), at any advance voting location. No reason needed.

                                              Source: Elections BC: key dates · Elections BC: ways to vote

                                            • ◆ Sunday, October 18

                                              Last day to request a vote-by-mail package online or by phone (1-800-661-8683).

                                              Source: Elections BC: key dates · Elections BC: ways to vote

                                            • ◆ Saturday, October 24

                                              Final Voting Day, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. (Pacific time). Elections BC must receive vote-by-mail packages by 8 p.m.

                                              Source: Elections BC: key dates · Elections BC: ways to vote
