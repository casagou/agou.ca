## What this site's form collects and why
- **Event RSVP:** your name, email, and, if you choose, your phone and how many guests you are bringing. The campaign may contact you by email, phone or text about the event and the campaign.
## Volunteering, lawn signs and donations
Volunteer sign-ups, lawn-sign requests and donations now go through the Conservative Party of BC's website. This site no longer collects them. The party's [privacy policy](https://conservativebc.ca/privacy-policy/) applies to what you enter there.
## Forms this site used before 2 October 2026
- **Volunteer:** your first and last name, email, phone and, if you chose, your address. The Joachim Agou campaign (Conservative Party of BC, Victoria–Beacon Hill) may contact you by email, phone or text about volunteering.
- **Nominate:** your full name, residential street address, phone, and, if you chose, your email, the best time to reach you and the signing sessions you could attend. The campaign contacted you about signing the nomination paper. Your information is used only for this nomination.
- **Lawn sign:** your name, email, phone, address, where the sign would go, delivery notes, and your confirmation that you are the owner or have permission. The campaign may contact you by email, phone or text about your lawn sign and the campaign.

You can ask the campaign to stop contacting you at any time. To ask, email [joachim@agou.ca](mailto:joachim@agou.ca) or call [672-922-7017](tel:+16729227017).
